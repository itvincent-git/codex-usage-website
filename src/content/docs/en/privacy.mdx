## What you provide on first use

No Codex Usage Desktop account or OpenAI or LiteLLM API key is required. The app reads existing logs in your local Codex home and reuses local Codex authentication for live limits.

## What stays on your computer

Source logs are read locally. The app does not upload, share, or modify session logs. Aggregated statistics are stored in a SQLite cache in the operating system's app data directory.

Sessions can contain sensitive prompts, source code, commands, and working directories. Seeing these in the app does not mean they were uploaded.

## When the app connects to the network

- **Live limits** use existing local Codex authentication to query ChatGPT directly. Session logs are not attached.
- **Model pricing** retrieves public prices and caches them locally for estimates and the catalog.
- **Other resources** include public font files, quota forecasts, and update information.

These requests do not include session logs or usage analytics. Local log processing does not mean every feature works without a network. Limits and updates need their services, and the offline pricing catalog may contain only limited built-in data.

## Before a screenshot, export, or issue report

1. Crop screenshots to the relevant area and mask accounts, project names, absolute paths, and session titles.
2. Check expanded activity for prompts, code, commands, searches, and filenames.
3. Inspect all exported worksheets or the full document.
4. Include only necessary diagnostics in reports. Do not attach complete logs, login credentials, or keys.

The app does not upload logs, but you control the audience of files and screenshots you share yourself. Inspect the [source code](https://github.com/itvincent-git/codex-usage-desktop) or follow [troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting/) for further investigation.
