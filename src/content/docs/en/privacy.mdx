## What you provide on first use

The app reads your local Codex logs and can reuse an existing ChatGPT sign-in for live limits. Codex Usage Desktop requires neither separate registration nor an API Key to read local usage.

If you use [Accounts](/docs/accounts/) to save ChatGPT accounts or API Keys, those credentials are stored as plaintext on your computer, with file permissions that restrict access. Keep credential files out of shared backups and issue reports.

## What stays on your computer

Source logs are read locally. The app does not upload, share, or modify session logs. Aggregated statistics are stored in a SQLite cache in the operating system's app data directory.

Sessions can contain sensitive prompts, source code, commands, and working directories. Seeing these in the app does not mean they were uploaded.

## When the app connects to the network

- **Live limits and account status** are requested directly from ChatGPT using the relevant local sign-in. The Accounts page can also refresh saved ChatGPT accounts in the background. These requests contain authentication data and omit session logs.
- **Model pricing** retrieves public prices and caches them locally for estimates and the catalog.
- **Session credits** are off by default. If enabled, the app uses your ChatGPT sign-in to send session IDs directly to ChatGPT and caches the returned amounts locally per account. Session contents stay on your device.
- **Other resources** include public font files and reset announcements and forecasts from codex-reset.com.

Live limits and update checks require a network connection. The offline pricing catalog may contain only limited built-in data.

## Before a screenshot, export, or issue report

1. Crop screenshots to the relevant area and mask accounts, project names, absolute paths, and session titles.
2. Check expanded activity for prompts, code, commands, searches, and filenames.
3. Inspect all exported worksheets or the full document.
4. Include only necessary diagnostics in reports. Do not attach complete logs, login credentials, or keys.

The app does not upload logs, but you control the audience of files and screenshots you share yourself. Inspect the [source code](https://github.com/itvincent-git/codex-usage-desktop) or follow [troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting/) for further investigation.
