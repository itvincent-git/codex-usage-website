## What reset information tells you

The limits card shows current account headroom. Reset information provides context: when changes were observed, whether an official announcement exists, and whether available reset credits expire. Read these as separate kinds of information.

## A useful reading order

1. Check the current balance, window, and countdown in the [limits card](/docs/quota/).
2. Review the latest official token-reset announcement and recent reset events.
3. Browse the 30-day announcement history when looking back.
4. Compare daily quota balance changes observed in local session logs.

An announcement does not prove the same change has already reached your account. Check live limits for your current state. Local history only includes observations actually present in the logs.

## Credits, expiration, and forecasts

When available, reset-credit details include expiration times. Distinguish a credit's expiration from a 5-hour or weekly window's reset time.

Forecasts provide context and may change with new information. They are not guarantees. A missing forecast does not mean no reset will occur, and missing history may simply mean there was no local quota snapshot at that time.

## Investigate a sudden increase

Check live limits again, then compare window reset times, reset events, and announcements. Low token usage on a particular day does not by itself prove extra quota was granted.

To examine work around the change, open [Sessions](/docs/sessions/) for the same period and inspect any recorded quota readings.
