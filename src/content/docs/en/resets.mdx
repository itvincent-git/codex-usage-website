## Reset information

The app can show the latest official token-reset announcement, recent reset events, and a 30-day announcement history. It can also display daily quota balance changes observed in session logs.

When available, reset-credit details include expiration times. Forecasts are estimates and may change as quota information changes. A missing forecast does not mean a reset will not occur.

For your current account limit and countdown, use the [quota guide](/docs/quota/).
