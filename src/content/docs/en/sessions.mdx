## Find a session

Browse sessions by day to see the day's token total. Search by title, project, or model. Each session can show total, input, cached, and output tokens, plus estimated cost. When the local logs contain enough quota readings, the list also estimates how much of the 5-hour and weekly limits that session used. These percentages are estimates, not live remaining balances.

## Inspect activity

Open a session to inspect its recorded timeline. Depending on the log, it may include commands and tools, web searches and results, patches and diffs, long-running commands, subagent hierarchy, and remaining quota changes. Links to raw JSONL can help when you need the source record.

The detail view can only show information present in the local Codex log. Session logs may contain sensitive prompts, code, and commands, so take care when sharing screenshots or exports.
