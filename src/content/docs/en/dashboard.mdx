## What the dashboard shows

The dashboard summarizes total tokens and estimated cost for the selected date range. It separates input, output, and cached tokens and shows cache hit rate, average daily usage, and daily or monthly trends.

## Explore a time range

Change the date range to compare recent work with a longer period. Open the Daily or Monthly view to find spikes, then use Project, Model, and Sessions to understand what contributed to them.

Usage comes from local Codex logs. If the latest session is missing, refresh the app and confirm that Codex CLI wrote a session log. Cost is an estimate, not a bill.
